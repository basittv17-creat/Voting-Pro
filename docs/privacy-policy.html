Privacy Policy for Voting Pro

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Voting Pro is a poster-making application that allows users to create, edit, save, and share voting and competition posters. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Photos and Poster Content

Voting Pro allows you to select photos from your device and use them in poster designs. Photos and poster content are processed on your device for editing and export features. The current app does not provide a developer-operated server for uploading your posters.

2. Information Collection

The current version of Voting Pro does not require users to create an account. The app's editing features store project settings locally on your device so your work can be restored. We do not receive your selected photos or poster designs through these features.

Your device, operating system, app store, or any third-party service you choose to use may process information under its own privacy policy.

3. Permissions

The app may request access needed to select photos, save finished posters, or share images. These permissions are used to provide the feature you choose to use.

4. Saving and Sharing

When you save a poster, the image is saved using the available features on your device. If you choose to share a poster with another app or service, that service's terms and privacy policy will apply. Please review the destination before sharing personal content.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Project settings are stored locally by the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may delete locally stored project settings. Images exported to your device or sent to another service may need to be deleted separately.

6. Data Security

We aim to keep the app's data handling limited to the features described in this policy. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your device and be careful when sharing posters.

7. Children's Privacy

Voting Pro is a general-purpose poster-making tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account to use its current poster-editing features.

8. Third-Party Services

Saving, photo selection, and sharing may use services provided by your device or other applications. These services are not controlled by Voting Pro and may handle information according to their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the official Voting Pro project repository:

https://github.com/basittv17-creat/Voting-Pro

Please do not post private photos, passwords, or other sensitive information in public project discussions.
